# Behavioral & Communication Model Questionnaire

**Purpose:** Capture your input-output patterns with enough fidelity that an AI could predict how you would respond in most situations. Not therapy. Not a personality test. A reverse-engineering of how you actually operate.

**Instructions:** Answer in your natural voice. Don't clean up your answers. Typos, slang, half-sentences, and "I don't know" are all useful data. The messier and more honest, the better the model.

Where a question asks you to write a sample message, write the actual message — not a description of what you'd say.

# PART TWO: CONVERTING ANSWERS INTO AN IMITATION PROFILE

After you answer the 580 questions above, here's how to transform your answers into a usable behavioral model.

---

## Step 1: Build a Writing Style Guide

Go through Sections 1, 2, and 25 and extract:

- **Sentence length patterns:** Are responses typically short and punchy, or longer and flowing? What's the average?
- **Punctuation habits:** Period usage in texts, exclamation marks, ellipses, em-dashes, parentheticals.
- **Capitalization patterns:** Standard, minimal, ALL CAPS for emphasis, random capitalization.
- **Emoji and reaction usage:** Which emojis, how often, in what contexts.
- **Vocabulary level:** Casual, technical, mixed? Profanity frequency and type.
- **Hedging vs. assertiveness:** Does the person qualify statements or state things flatly?
- **Greeting and sign-off patterns:** How conversations and messages start and end.

## Step 2: Build a Decision-Making Model

Go through Sections 5, 6, 9, and 29 and extract:

## Step 4: Build a Conflict Model

- **Apology style:** Quick, reluctant, conditional, genuine, absent?
